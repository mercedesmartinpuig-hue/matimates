Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026 · Mati · Primary Maths (es.matimates.mati)

In one sentenceMati does not collect personal data from children or send it to any server. Progress stays on the device. Subscriptions are handled by Google Play on the adult's account. With Mati Plus only, the adult's email is used to recover the parent PIN.

2. Children's data: none collected

  • The name requested at the start is a local nickname so Mati can address the child. It is stored on the device and never transmitted.
  • We do not collect email, phone number, photos, date of birth, school, location or device identifiers.
  • There is no registration, login or cloud sync of the child's profile.
  • The weekly league uses fictional names and scores generated on the device. Nobody competes against a real person.

Deleting the app or clearing its data permanently removes all of this. We hold no copy, and the app disables Android automatic backup.

Adult's email to recover the PIN

With Mati Plus, the adult who sets the PIN enters their email address. It is stored only on the device. Only when the adult taps "Forgot your PIN?" does the app send that email and a 6-digit code to our sending service, which delivers it and keeps no copy. Legal basis: performance of the service requested by the adult (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). A child's email is never requested.

4. Children and advertising

  • No ads. Mati contains no advertising networks.
  • No tracking analytics. No SDK assigns an identifier to the device or child.
  • Adult purchases only. Subscriptions sit behind a parent PIN and use the adult's Google Play account.
  • No chat or social features.

5. United States (COPPA)

Mati is directed to children under 13 and does not collect, use or disclose personal information from them. Parents can contact us at any time with questions.

6. Third parties

  • Google Play handles downloads, updates and subscriptions under Google's privacy policy. We receive subscription status, not the buyer's email or payment method.
  • Website hosting may log connection data for security. This website sets no cookies.
  • PIN code email (only when the adult asks): Cloudflare, Inc. runs our sending service and Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc.) delivers the email from servers in the European Union (Ireland). They act as processors and may keep technical delivery logs for a limited time under their own policies.
  • We never sell or share data with advertisers or data brokers.

7. Changes

If Mati ever starts processing personal data, this page will be updated first, the app will notify you, and consent will be requested where required.
